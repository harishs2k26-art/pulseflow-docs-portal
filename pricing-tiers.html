What counts as an event?

One accepted envelope counts as one event, regardless of the number of indexed attributes. A batch of 25 accepted envelopes counts as 25. Rejected requests, identical idempotent resubmissions within the 24-hour deduplication window and outbound delivery attempts do not count again. A new event with a new identity does count, even if its body resembles an earlier one.

In this model overages are prorated to the exact event count, not rounded up to whole million-event blocks. Monthly allowances reset at 00:00 UTC on the first day. Retention is a rolling window and does not reset with billing.

Budget and data controls

  • Set warnings at 50%, 80% and 100% of the included allowance.
  • Choose a hard ingestion cap or opt-in overages before sending production-like traffic.
  • Sample routine successes while retaining failures, and record the sampling policy alongside reports.
  • Apply attribute allowlists; higher spending limits never justify storing credentials or personal information.

Frequently asked billing questions

Does a retry cost another event?

Not when the original accepted request is reused with the same idempotency key and body within 24 hours. A fresh logical observation is billed separately.

Can these plans be purchased?

No. Everything here is fictional test content. The buttons, forms and examples elsewhere never initiate payments or accounts.
